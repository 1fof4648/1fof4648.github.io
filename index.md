## ✨ What mc-download-manager Does

The app is built around a small number of features that each solve a real annoyance.

**Parallel-connection downloads.** Instead of pulling a file through one connection, mc-download-manager opens several at once and stitches the pieces together. Large files — installers, video files, archives, disk images — finish noticeably faster on most home connections.

**Pause and resume.** You can stop a download in the middle, close your laptop, come back an hour later, and continue from where you left off. No restarting from zero.

**Browser integration.** A companion extension for Chrome, Brave, and Safari hands download links straight to the app. When you click a download link in your browser, mc-download-manager takes over instead of the browser saving it silently into a folder you will never find again.

**Video downloads.** Through yt-dlp, the app can grab video files from many sites that do not offer a normal "save" button. Paste a video page address into the app and it will offer the available formats.

**English and Turkish interface.** Switch languages inside the app settings. All main labels and messages are translated.

**Open source.** The full source code is public. Anyone can read it, report bugs, or suggest improvements.

## 🧩 Setting Up Browser Integration

The browser extension is what makes everyday use feel effortless. Setup takes about a minute.

1. Open mc-download-manager and leave it running in the background.
2. Inside the app, look for the browser integration or extension section in settings.
3. The app will show you the extension it needs. Install it into your browser when prompted.
4. If your browser asks for permission to "read and change data on sites you visit", accept it — that permission is what allows the extension to notice download links.
5. Restart your browser once so the extension loads properly.
6. Test it: find any file link on a website and click it. The download should now appear inside mc-download-manager instead of your browser's download list.

If downloads still go to the browser, open the browser's extension settings and make sure the mc-download-manager extension is switched on and not disabled.

## 🎬 Saving Videos

Video saving works through yt-dlp, which is bundled with the app. Copy the address of a video page from your browser's address bar, open mc-download-manager, choose the video download option, and paste the address. The app will list the formats it found. Pick one, choose a save folder, and start.

Higher-resolution formats produce much larger files. If you only need to watch something once, the smaller options are usually enough.

## 🌍 Language Settings

Open the settings area inside the app and look for a language selector. Choose English or Türkçe. The change applies immediately. If a label still appears in the other language, close and reopen the app once.

## 🛠️ Common Questions

**The download page looks complicated.** It is just a list. Newest version at the top, files attached below it. Pick the top version, then pick the file.

**My antivirus flagged the file.** Small open-source projects are sometimes flagged because they are not widely known yet. You can verify the file yourself on the releases page, where checksums are published for each version.

**The app opens but downloads do not start.** Check that you have free space on the drive you are saving to, and that your internet connection is working in your browser. Then try a small test file first.

**Can I change where files are saved?** Yes. The save location is set in the app settings and can be changed at any time.

**Do I need to keep the app open?** Yes, while a download is running. Closing the app pauses active downloads, which you can resume next time.
